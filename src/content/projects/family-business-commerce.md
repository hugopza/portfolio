## Context

This project is grounded in a real business environment, where decisions are influenced by daily operations rather than idealized product design.

The work involves translating informal requirements from non-technical stakeholders into a usable and maintainable e-commerce system.

---

## Implementation

The implementation focuses on configuring and adapting Shopify to match the business:

- product catalogue structured based on real inventory
- category organization aligned with how products are sold in-store
- customization of theme and UI where necessary
- integration of essential e-commerce flows (product, cart, checkout)

The goal is not to build a complex system, but to deliver a reliable and usable online extension of the physical store.

---

## Reflection

The main challenge is not technical complexity, but alignment with real-world constraints.

Working with an existing business forces tradeoffs that are often absent in portfolio projects: simplicity over flexibility, usability over completeness, and adaptation over ideal architecture.

This project reinforces that building useful products often means fitting into reality, not redesigning it.
